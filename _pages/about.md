 I'm a PhD student pursuing my doctoral studies at [IIIT - Bangalore](https://www.iiitb.ac.in/), in Computational Topology under the guidance of [Dr. Amit Chattopadhyay](https://www.iiitb.ac.in/faculty/amit-chattopadhyay). Beyond the world of academia, I find solace and joy in exploring the beauty of mountains, often capturing moments through my lens, a collection of which you can find on my [Captures](/portfolio/) page. I'm captivated by the world of numbers, exploring the subtle patterns of randomness and structures — a dead give away as I do work in topology.

This site is where those threads come together: writing on the [blog](/year-archive/) about things like the architecture of the internet or [interactive tools I've built for analyzing structured workout data](/posts/2026/03/gpx-workout-analyzer/), photos from the mountains in [Captures](/portfolio/), and short clips over on [Videos](/videos/).
